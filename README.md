📊 Power BI -- 2015 Product Lines Sales Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze sales
performance, product lines, orders, quantities, and profitability using
2015 sales data.

The dashboard is designed to be responsive and interactive, allowing
users to explore and generate insights based on City and Product
Line selections.

🖼️ Dashboard Preview



📌 Project Overview

This project presents a visual analysis of 2015 sales data through an
interactive Power BI dashboard. It combines multiple visualizations and
filters to provide a quick overview of business performance and allow
users to drill into product- and location-level information.

The dashboard can be used to explore:

Overall profit and order performance

Sales by product line

Sales and profit by individual products

Quantity ordered across product lines

Product-level quantity and sales contribution

City-based sales and product-line insights

Geographic distribution of sales data

Detailed product-level performance

🎯 Dashboard Objectives

Analyze overall sales performance for 2015.

Understand which product lines contribute to sales and quantity
ordered.

Compare product-level sales and profit.

Explore business performance across different cities.

Identify products with higher sales, profit, or order quantities.

Provide an interactive dashboard for quick business analysis.

Enable users to obtain filtered insights without manually modifying
the underlying data.

📊 Key KPIs

The dashboard includes high-level KPI cards for:

KPI                 Description

Total Profit    Overall profit represented in the dataset
Total Orders    Total number of orders
Sales of 2015   Total sales generated during 2015

The displayed dashboard currently shows approximately ₹8.85M Total
Profit, 35.58K Total Orders, and 4M Sales.

🔍 Interactive Features

City Filter

The City slicer allows users to select a specific city and analyze
the corresponding sales and product performance.

Product Line Filter

The Product Lines slicer allows users to focus the dashboard on a
particular product category.

Product-Level Analysis

The dashboard provides product-level information including:

Sales

Profit

Quantity Ordered

Product Line Analysis

Visuals provide comparisons of sales and quantity ordered across
different product lines.

Geographic Analysis

A map visual provides a geographic view of the available city/location
data.

Dynamic Filtering

The visuals are connected through Power BI interactions, so selecting a
city or product line dynamically updates the relevant dashboard
components.

📈 Visualizations Used

The dashboard contains multiple visualizations, including:

KPI Cards -- Total Profit, Total Orders, and 2015 Sales

Pie/Donut Chart -- Sales distribution by product line

Bar Charts -- Sales and quantity ordered by product/product line

Map Visual -- Geographic distribution by city

Treemap -- Quantity ordered by product line

Detailed Table/Matrix -- Product-wise profit and quantity
information

Slicers -- City and Product Line filters

🚀 How to Use

Download or clone this repository.

Open the .pbix file in Microsoft Power BI Desktop.

If required, update the dataset/file path in Power Query.

Refresh the data.

Use the City and Product Lines slicers to interact with the
dashboard.

Explore the charts, map, KPI cards, and detailed product table to
generate insights.

📁 Dataset

The dashboard was created using a 2015 sales dataset containing
information used for analyzing products, product lines, orders,
quantities, sales, profit, and city/location-level performance.

The dataset used for this project is included in this repository.
